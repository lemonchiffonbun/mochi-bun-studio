Stages

This is how an art commission goes. Every stage ends with your approval before I move on to the next one.

Sketching

What I do
Draw two rough sketches based on your references and ideas.
What you do
Choose the sketch you like best and tell me what to adjust.

Line Art

What I do
Turn the approved sketch into clean line art.
What you do
Review it and approve it before I start coloring.

Coloring

What I do

What you do
Approve the colors before I add the final details.

Final Touches

What I do

General Terms
  • Reference photos must be shared with consent of the person depicted.
  • No inappropriate requests will be accepted.
  • Privacy is respected; do not ask for personal information.
  • I reserve the right to decline service if terms are violated or communication is unprofessional.
  • Payment must be made upfront after the initial sketch approval; no refunds after this stage.
  • No third‑party involvement; all communication remains between client and artist.
Commission Process
  1. Payment is made upfront after the initial agreement.
  2. Two rough sketches are provided; the client approves one.
  3. Line art is created and must be approved before continuing.
  4. Coloring is completed and must be approved before continuing.
  5. Final details, lighting, and shading are added; once approved, the commission is complete.
Things to Keep in Mind
  • Standard deadline is 5 days; shorter deadlines increase the price.
  • Redos are possible after the sketch stage if deadlines are extended.
  • Refunds are only available before sketch approval.
  • Feedback and adjustments are welcome to ensure satisfaction.
  • Physical features will not be altered unless requested.
  • Level of stylization is up to the client.
Deluxe Gift Website and Photos
  • The Deluxe Gift Website recognizes the face of the person you're gifting it to, so I need photos of that person from as many angles as possible.
  • Your photos are used only to build your gift. I do not store photos or share them with third parties.
  • Recognition works best with clear, well-lit photos. Results can vary with lighting and camera quality.
  • The listed price is a starting range and can go higher depending on what you want the site to do.
Privacy Policy

All client information, references, and communication are kept confidential. Personal details such as addresses, phone numbers, or private data will never be requested, stored, or shared. Only art‑related references are accepted, and any files provided are used solely for the commission. Finished works may be displayed in my portfolio unless the client requests otherwise. No client information will be sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties. Communication remains professional and limited to the project.

Payment Policy

Payments are accepted through PayPal (preferred) or other agreed methods. An invoice will be sent once the initial sketch is approved, and full payment must be completed before continuing to line art. Refunds are only available before sketch approval. All prices are listed in MXN with approximate EUR and USD conversions for clarity. Clients agree to complete payment promptly to avoid delays.

Finished artwork will be delivered digitally through Google Drive or email, unless otherwise agreed. Clients are responsible for providing clear references and timely feedback during approval stages. The artist retains the right to display completed works in a portfolio unless privacy is requested. The artist is not liable for misuse, alteration, or redistribution of the artwork once delivered.
